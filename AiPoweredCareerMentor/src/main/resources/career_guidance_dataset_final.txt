
SECTION 1: AFTER 10TH STANDARD – CHOOSING STREAMS

1. PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
Description: Ideal for students interested in engineering, technology, and pure sciences. Builds analytical, mathematical, and logical skills.
Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, optional subject (Computer Science/Physical Education/others).
Possible Careers:
- Engineering (B.Tech/BE)
- Architecture (B.Arch)
- Merchant Navy
- Pilot
- Defence Services
- Actuarial Science
- Data Science / AI
Entrance Exams:
- JEE Main & Advanced (Engineering)
- NATA (Architecture)
- NDA (Defence)
- CUET (Universities)
- SAT (International)
Career Path Example: PCM → B.Tech in Computer Science → Job in Software Engineering → Growth into AI/ML Specialist.

2. PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
Description: For students inclined towards medical sciences, research, or biotechnology.
Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, optional subject.
Possible Careers:
- Doctor (MBBS, BDS, BHMS, BAMS)
- Biotechnology
- Biomedical Engineering
- Genetics
- Veterinary Sciences
- Nursing
- Physiotherapy
Entrance Exams:
- NEET-UG (Medical)
- AIIMS
- CUET for B.Sc.
Career Path Example: PCB → MBBS → Doctor → Surgeon/Consultant → Hospital Administration.

3. Commerce
Description: Suitable for careers in business, finance, economics, and entrepreneurship.
Subjects: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics/Information Practices, English.
Possible Careers:
- Chartered Accountant (CA)
- Company Secretary (CS)
- Financial Analyst
- Investment Banker
- BBA / MBA
- B.Com + M.Com
Entrance Exams:
- CA Foundation
- CUET
- IPMAT (Integrated MBA)
- SAT
Career Path Example: Commerce → B.Com + CA → Financial Analyst → CFO.

4. Arts/Humanities
Description: Suitable for students with interests in social sciences, languages, and creative fields.
Subjects: Political Science, Sociology, Psychology, History, Geography, Literature.
Possible Careers:
- Civil Services
- Lawyer
- Journalist
- Psychologist
- Historian
- Designer
- Educator
Entrance Exams:
- UPSC
- CLAT
- CUET
- NIFT (Design)
Career Path Example: Arts → BA in Psychology → MA in Psychology → Clinical Psychologist.

SECTION 2: AFTER 12TH STANDARD – DEGREE COURSES

1. B.Tech / BE (Engineering)
Description: 4-year degree focusing on technology and problem-solving.
Specializations: Computer Science, AI & Data Science, Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Chemical.
Entrance Exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, State CETs, BITSAT, VITEEE.
Career Opportunities:
- Software Developer
- AI Engineer
- Robotics Engineer
- Government jobs like IES.
Career Trajectory: B.Tech → Job → M.Tech or MBA → Senior Manager or Entrepreneur.

2. MBBS / BDS / BAMS / BHMS (Medical Degrees)
Description: Specialized degrees to become medical professionals.
Entrance Exams: NEET-UG, AIIMS.
Career Opportunities:
- Surgeon
- Physician
- Dentist
- Medical Researcher
- Healthcare Administrator.
Career Trajectory: MBBS → Post-graduation → Doctor in Specialty → Hospital Director.

3. B.Sc (Bachelor of Science)
Description: Pure sciences for research, academics, or applied science careers.
Specializations: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths, Statistics, Biotechnology, Computer Science.
Entrance Exams: CUET, SAT.
Career Opportunities:
- Scientist
- Data Scientist
- Statistician
- Academic Professor
Career Trajectory: B.Sc → M.Sc → Ph.D → Scientist or University Professor.

4. BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
Description: Undergraduate program for management and entrepreneurship.
Entrance Exams: IPMAT, CUET, DU-JAT.
Career Opportunities:
- Business Manager
- HR Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Entrepreneur
Career Trajectory: BBA → MBA → Senior Management → CEO.

5. B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce)
Description: Degree focused on accounting, finance, and economics.
Entrance Exams: CUET, CA Foundation (parallel).
Career Opportunities:
- Chartered Accountant
- Financial Analyst
- Auditor
- Tax Consultant
Career Trajectory: B.Com → CA or MBA → Finance Manager → CFO.

6. BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
Description: Computer-focused degree for IT careers.
Entrance Exams: CUET, State Level Tests.
Career Opportunities:
- Software Developer
- Data Analyst
- Game Developer
- Cloud Engineer
Career Trajectory: BCA → MCA → Software Architect → CTO.

7. BA (Bachelor of Arts)
Description: Academic degree for humanities and social sciences.
Specializations: Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, English Literature, History.
Entrance Exams: CUET, State Universities.
Career Opportunities:
- Civil Services
- Journalist
- Teacher
- Social Worker
Career Trajectory: BA → MA → Civil Services or Academic Career.

SECTION 6: STREAM SUGGESTION BASED ON QUIZ SCORES

The following rules map quiz scores to streams. The quiz will have a total score of 100, divided equally across four areas:
- Logic & Mathematics
- Biology & Life Sciences
- Business & Finance
- Creativity & Social Skills

SCORE RANGES AND SUGGESTIONS:

1. Logic & Mathematics:
- Score 70-100: PCM, BCA, B.Tech
- Score 40-69: PCM with backup options like Commerce or BCA
- Score <40: Avoid PCM, focus on other domains.

2. Biology & Life Sciences:
- Score 70-100: PCB, MBBS, B.Sc (Biology/Biotechnology)
- Score 40-69: Consider Biology with Commerce or Arts.
- Score <40: Avoid biology-intensive streams.

3. Business & Finance:
- Score 70-100: Commerce, BBA, B.Com
- Score 40-69: Commerce with Arts or BBA with backup options.
- Score <40: Focus on other areas instead of pure commerce.

4. Creativity & Social Skills:
- Score 70-100: Arts, BA (Psychology, Sociology, Political Science), Design Courses (NIFT, NID).
- Score 40-69: Arts or a mix with Commerce.
- Score <40: Avoid purely creative streams.

FINAL SUGGESTION LOGIC:
- The highest scoring category will be the primary stream recommendation.
- If two categories are close (difference <10 points), suggest a combination, e.g., PCM + Commerce.
- Example:
    Logic & Math = 80, Biology = 45, Business = 60, Creativity = 30
    Recommended Stream: PCM as primary, Commerce as secondary option.



--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 7: USER PREFERENCE WEIGHTAGE IN STREAM SUGGESTION

In addition to quiz scores, the student can specify a personal preference for their desired stream.
This personal preference will be given **more weight** than quiz scores while generating recommendations.

WEIGHTAGE LOGIC:
- User Preference Weightage: 60%
- Quiz Scores Weightage: 40%

WORKFLOW:
1. If the user specifies a preferred stream (e.g., PCM, PCB, Commerce, Arts), add 60 points to that stream.
2. Combine quiz score percentage for each category with the user preference.
3. The stream with the highest combined score will be recommended.

EXAMPLE:
- User Preference: PCM
- Quiz Scores: 
  - Logic & Math = 70
  - Biology = 30
  - Business = 50
  - Creativity = 40

CALCULATION:
- PCM total = (User Preference 60) + (Logic & Math 70 * 0.4) = 60 + 28 = 88
- PCB total = Biology 30 * 0.4 = 12
- Commerce total = Business 50 * 0.4 = 20
- Arts total = Creativity 40 * 0.4 = 16

FINAL RECOMMENDATION:
- PCM is selected as the top recommendation because it has the highest weighted score (88).

EDGE CASES:
- If no user preference is given, rely entirely on quiz scores (100% weightage).
- If two streams have equal combined scores, recommend both as co-primary options.
